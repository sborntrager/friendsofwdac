Community snapshot

Friends of WDAC is a local support system for aquatic access.

Friends of WDAC is a community association of supporters who advocate, fundraise, and volunteer to sustain aquatic programs in Cottage Grove.

300+ patrons and supporters connected to the mission.
70+ years of community history tied to local swim access.
All ages from swim lessons to senior wellness programs.
"The pool is where our kids learn confidence and our seniors keep moving."
