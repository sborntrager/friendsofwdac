Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to usable, inclusive access for every visitor.

Commitment

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Friends of WDAC is committed to providing a website that is usable for people of all abilities. Our goal is to align with WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards where feasible.

Accessibility features in this build

  • Skip-to-content links and consistent page landmarks.
  • Keyboard-focus indicators for navigation, buttons, and interactive links.
  • Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile use.
  • Reduced-motion support for reveal and animation behavior.
  • Labeled form controls and status messaging for subscription actions.

Ongoing work

  • Manual contrast checks across image-heavy sections.
  • Continuous keyboard navigation testing across all pages.
  • Content and readability tuning for small-screen and low-vision contexts.
  • Progressive remediation of legacy downloadable PDFs.

Latest contrast notes: contrast audit record (March 30, 2026).

Known limitations

Some legacy third-party or archival PDF documents may not yet be fully optimized for screen readers. If you need accessible alternatives for any document, contact us and we will provide support.

You can also use the PDF accessibility options page for current alternatives and support steps.

Need help or want to report an issue?

Email contact@FriendsofWDAC.org or mail P.O. Box 51, Cottage Grove, OR 97424 with:

  • The page URL or document name
  • A description of the issue
  • Your preferred format or support method

We aim to acknowledge requests within 5 business days and resolve issues as quickly as possible.
