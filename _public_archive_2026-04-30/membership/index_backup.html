Who are our members?

Friends of the WDAC members span all ages, and include those who attend aqua-exercise classes and learn-to-swim lessons, therapy pool users, lap swimmers, recreational swim participants and families. Friends also include current participants and alumni from the Cottage Grove High School swim and water athletics program.

How to join

It's easy to add your voice to ours. Simply complete and return the "Become a Friend" form, selecting one or more ways to contribute. There are several ways to do so - stay informed, volunteer to attend events or meetings, lend your expertise or leadership, and/or donate a dollar amount you are comfortable giving. You choose how best to participate.

Printed / check / mail

  • Download the PDF form to your computer (fillable in most PDF readers).
  • Save the completed form (we recommend adding your name to the file name).
  • Print and mail the form with a check or money order (if donating).

Checks / Money Orders: payable to "BYB (Friends of WDAC)".

Mail to: Friends of the WDAC, P.O. Box 51, Cottage Grove, OR 97424

Online form / electronic payment

Use BYB's form via GiveButter.com, a secure online fund-raising app specifically designed for nonprofit organizations. This platform allows you to pay with many common electronic methods, from Venmo and PayPal to credit / debit cards.
