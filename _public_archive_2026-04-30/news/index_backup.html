Advocacy + impact

Highlights from budget advocacy, awareness building, equipment upgrades, and ongoing work.

Highlights

Work from 2025-2026 spans advocacy, awareness, operational support, and long-term organization building.

8 key updates 2025-2026 coverage Nearly $7,000 in equipment support
  1. Mar-May 2025 Budget advocacy

    Initial Budget Advocacy: Friends successfully campaigned for continued City of Cottage Grove funding of WDAC community programs for the 2025-26 fiscal year. Friends gathered contact information for about 250 swimmers and aqua-exercisers to keep them informed about budget deliberations and opportunities to communicate benefits of WDAC community programs. The effort culminated in more than 75 WDAC community swimmers and exercisers attending the final meeting of the city council budget committee, which unanimously approved continued city financial support.

  2. Mar-Jul 2025 Community awareness

    Awareness Building: Friends designed and sold 73 "I love my community pool" T-shirts. About 35 supporters wore these shirts and marched behind a matching banner in the Spirit of Bohemia Mining Days Parade. Friends also attended local Art Walks to share information about WDAC pools and their value to the community.

  3. Fall 2025 Equipment

    Equipment Upgrades: Friends donated nearly $7,000 to purchase a commercial steam cleaner (reducing staff time spent scrubbing the pool deck) and wave-dampening competition-quality lane lines that benefit both lap swimmers and competitive swimming.

  4. 2025-2026 Program operations

    Staffing support: Friends encouraged lifeguard training and credential renewals to help cover shifts vital to community programs, and helped recruit timers for Cottage Grove High School competition meets.

  5. Oct 2025-Jan 2026 Organization

    Nonprofit status: Friends organized as a sponsored project of Be Your Best Cottage Grove (BYB), enabling tax-deductible donations. The agreement was signed mid-January 2026.

  6. 2025-2026 Recognition

    Bud Taylor recognition: Friends supported alumni efforts to recognize Coach Bud Taylor and ongoing negotiations to name the lap / competition pool after him, along with a plaque and banner listing accomplishments.

  7. Q4 2025-Q1 2026 Communication

    Awareness + communication: Friends developed a project logo and established a website to inform, grow membership, and support advocacy.

  8. Winter 2025-Spring 2026 Budget advocacy

    Budget advocacy continues: Friends continues advocacy as the city deliberates funding for fiscal year 2026-2027, including meetings with school district and city leadership, informing pool users, and updating educational materials for city budget committee members.
