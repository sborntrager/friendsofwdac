Support our funding priorities

General donations, planned / legacy giving, and T-shirt orders help keep WDAC community programs strong.

Our funding priorities

These are mission-aligned areas we may support as funding allows:

  • Advocate for continued funding solutions for sustainable community use and programs at the WDAC.
  • Establish a long-term fiscal vehicle (such as an endowment) to support community use of WDAC into the future.
  • Scholarships to encourage and appreciate student lifeguards, and to support users who need financial assistance.
  • Specific equipment needs outside regular South Lane School District maintenance / operations.
  • New or enhanced community programs beyond current offerings.

Planned / legacy giving

Planned or legacy giving helps ensure advocacy efforts (including potential WDAC program funding) continue for the long-term. Planned giving includes:

  • Bequest in your will
  • Disbursement from a trust
  • Beneficiary proceeds from insurance policies, bank accounts, or retirement accounts
  • Proceeds from the sale of real estate, stock, or other non-liquid assets

Please consult your attorney and/or financial advisor for specific instructions and counsel. Friends of WDAC does not provide legal or financial advice.
