Community-backed support for WDAC programs and future stability.

Friends of WDAC formed in spring 2025 as City of Cottage Grove budget planning put annual WDAC support at risk. Nearly 300 WDAC patrons and supporters now work together to protect access, expand programs, and build long-term financial support for the pool.

About

Who we are and why we formed

Friends of WDAC represents a broad base of pool users and supporters across Cottage Grove. The group formed in spring 2025 when fiscal year planning suggested city support for WDAC community programs might be cut.

Mission and objectives

  • Preserve and strengthen community access to WDAC programs.
  • Operate as a 501(c)(3) effort with BYB Cottage Grove as sponsor.
  • Support operations through advocacy, fundraising, and volunteer engagement.

Health and well-being benefits of the community pool

  • Year-round physical activity options for all ages.
  • Low-impact fitness and therapy opportunities.
  • Water safety and swim skill development.
  • Social connection and family recreation in a shared civic space.

Membership

Who our members are and how to join

Members represent toddlers to seniors, including class and lesson attendees, therapy pool users, lap swimmers, recreational swimmers, families, and Cottage Grove High School swim program participants and alumni.

  • Complete the online membership form.
  • Choose your membership fee option.
  • Optional: identify if you are CGHS swim alumni and include years.
  • Printable form can be mailed with check to the Friends of WDAC P.O. Box.

Funding priorities and giving options

  • Scholarships for patrons who need fee assistance.
  • Scholarships that encourage and recognize student lifeguards.

  • Endowment growth for long-term WDAC fiscal support.
