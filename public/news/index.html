Highlights

Work from 2025-2026 spans advocacy, awareness, operational support, and long-term organization building.

8 key updates 2025-2026 coverage $7,000 in equipment support

Recent updates

  1. Mar-May 2025

    Budget advocacy

    Initial budget advocacy

    Successful campaign for continued City of Cottage Grove funding of WDAC community programs for the 2025-26 fiscal year, engaging 250+ community members and culminating in unanimous approval by the city budget committee.

  2. Mar-Jul 2025

    Community awareness

    Awareness building

    Friends designed and sold 73 "I love my community pool" T-shirts, marched in the Bohemia Mining Days Parade, and attended Art Walks to share WDAC information and community value.

  3. Fall 2025

    WDAC operations

    Equipment upgrades

    $7,000 donated to purchase a commercial steam cleaner and wave-dampening lane lines that support both lap swimmers and competitive swimming.

  4. 2025-2026

    WDAC operations

    Staffing support

    Friends partnered to fund lifeguard training, credential renewals, and recruit timers for Cottage Grove High School competition meets.

  5. Oct 2025-Jan 2026

    Organization

    BYB nonprofit status

    Friends organized as a sponsored project of Be Your Best Cottage Grove (BYB), enabling tax-deductible donations. Agreement signed mid-January 2026.

  6. 2025-2026

    Recognition

    Bud Taylor recognition

    Friends continue to support alumni efforts to honor Coach Bud Taylor and the naming of the lap / competition pool in his legacy.

  7. Q4 2025-Q1 2026

    Community awareness

    Awareness + communication

    New logo and website launched to inform, grow membership, and support ongoing advocacy.

  8. Winter 2025-Spring 2026

    Budget advocacy

    Budget advocacy continues

    Friends continue advocacy as the city deliberates funding for FY 2026-2027, including meetings with school district and city leadership and materials for budget committee members.

  9. Mar-Apr 2026

    WDAC operations

    Staffing support

    Funded professional development attendance for WDAC supervisory and operations staff.
