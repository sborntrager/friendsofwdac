Who are our members?

Friends of the WDAC members span all ages, and include those who attend aqua-exercise classes and learn-to-swim lessons, therapy pool users, lap swimmers, recreational swim participants and families. Friends also include current participants and alumni from the Cottage Grove High School swim and water polo athletics program. Finally, Friends include anyone who recognizes the value of the aquatic center to our community, and would like to support it.

How to join

It's easy to add your voice to ours. Simply complete and return the "Become a Friend" form, selecting one or more ways to contribute. There are several ways to do so - stay informed, volunteer to attend events or meetings, lend your expertise or leadership, and/or donate a dollar amount you are comfortable giving. You choose how best to participate.

Printed / check / mail

  • Download the PDF form to your computer (fillable in most PDF readers).
  • Save the completed form (we recommend adding your name to the file name).
  • Print and mail the form with a check or money order (if donating).

Checks / Money Orders: payable to "BYB (Friends of WDAC)".
