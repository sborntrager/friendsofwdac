Our funding priorities

Your support helps us strengthen the WDAC today and secure its future.

These are mission-aligned areas we hope to support as funding allows.

  • Advocate for continued funding solutions for sustainable community use and programs.
  • Establish a long-term fiscal vehicle (such as an endowment) to support community use of WDAC.
  • Scholarships to encourage and appreciate student lifeguards, and to support those who need financial assistance.
  • Specific equipment needs outside regular South Lane School District maintenance / operations.
  • New or enhanced community programs beyond current offerings.
