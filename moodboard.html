Rules & Notes

Credits: include photo credit either on-photo or at the bottom of each page (only for photos shown on that page).
Detail links: graphs/timelines should have an adjacent “Download PDF” link so labels are readable.
Permissions: avoid publishing any unvetted photos until cleared.
Attribution: newspaper-sourced assets likely need publication credit in the caption.
Accessibility: write alt text; don’t rely on images to carry critical info.
Permission watch: DanaMerryday_photo_Ciochetti-Pupke-Moon-Turay-Taylor.jpg was called out by the client as not permission-vetted yet.

Treatments Toolkit

Home tiles: use the pre-made faded BOX_* images as backgrounds so label text stays readable.
Quote teaser: Senior_Testimonial_Quote.jpg can be used as an optional graphic preview (not required).

Do / Don’t

Do

  • Use client-provided photos of the real place and real people.
  • Include a visible credit line (or page footer credits).
  • Show graph/timeline thumbnails with an adjacent PDF download link.

Don’t

  • Publish unvetted/uncleared photos.
  • Embed tiny graphs without a PDF link.
  • Use the faded BOX_* tile art as full-page hero images.

Voice & Writing

#voice

Voice traits

  • Warm, neighborly, and credible
  • Community-first and practical
  • Specific (names, dates, numbers) without sounding academic
  • Optimistic, not salesy

Preferred structure

  • Who / what / when in the first screen
  • Why it matters with 1–2 supporting facts
  • What you can do (donate, join, download, contact)

Sample headline

Protecting a Vital Community Asset

Keep paragraphs short, use meaningful subheads, and make downloads obvious.

Before: We are a nonprofit organization focused on aquatic programming.
After: We’re neighbors supporting WDAC programs for babies, athletes, and seniors—so the pool stays open and accessible.
